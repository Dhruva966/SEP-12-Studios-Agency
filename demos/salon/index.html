BEFORE YOUR VISIT

A few useful details.

What should I bring to a consultation?

Inspiration photos can help. Be ready to discuss your routine and any previous color or chemical treatments.

Can I book a color service straight away?

The team may need a consultation and appropriate testing first. Ask which steps your service requires.

Is my appointment confirmed here?

No. An inquiry starts the conversation; the salon must confirm your appointment directly.
