BEFORE YOUR VISIT

A few useful details.

What information should I include?

Your vehicle’s year, make, model, mileage, warning lights, and a description of when the symptoms occur.

Will work start without my approval?

The proposed inspection or repair, estimate, and timing should be agreed before you authorize the work.

Does this form confirm a service slot?

No. The shop must confirm availability directly. If the vehicle feels unsafe, arrange assistance instead of driving it.
