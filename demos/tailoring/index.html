BEFORE YOUR VISIT

A few useful details.

What should I bring to a fitting?

Bring the garment, the shoes you plan to wear with it, and any relevant underlayers.

How much will the work cost?

The fabric, construction, and changes needed affect the estimate. The work and price are agreed after assessment.

Can you work to an event date?

Include your deadline in your inquiry. The team must confirm the work and timing before you rely on a collection date.
