BEFORE YOUR VISIT

A few useful details.

What should I mention at drop-off?

Point out stains, repairs, delicate details, and any care preferences. Keep care labels attached where possible.

How long does cleaning take?

Timing depends on the items and treatment. Confirm collection timing with the shop when the care is agreed.

Will every stain come out?

Results depend on the fabric, stain, and previous treatment. Ask the team to assess the item before cleaning.
